Topological_Analysis is a Python library for analyzing interstitial sites in oxide and sulfide crystal structures, based on Voronoi analysis. It identifies possible interstitial sites for a given species, including original lattice sites, and provides filters for site selection along with a simple executable script. The script outputs CIF and CSV files showing the Voronoi radius and positions of interstitial sites, along with bond valence and other structural information. It relies on [Zeo++](http://www.zeoplusplus.org/) and [pymatgen](http://pymatgen.org/).

Reference: Xingfeng He<sup>1</sup>, Qiang Bai<sup>1</sup>, Yunsheng Liu, Adelaide M. Nolan, Chen Ling, Yifei Mo\*, "Crystal Structural Framework of Lithium Super-Ionic Conductors", [***Advanced Energy Materials*** 9, 1902078 (2019)](https://doi.org/10.1002/aenm.201902078)
