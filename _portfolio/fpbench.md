FPBench decomposes the error of machine-learned foundation potentials into application-oriented components, and hosts a public leaderboard for comparing model performance.

Reference: Kiyan Amirian, Ramanuja Srinivasan Saravanan, Felix Adams, Charles E. Schwarz, Yifei Mo\*, "FPBench: Application-Oriented Error Decomposition for Foundation Potentials", [arXiv:2609.05714](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05714)
