This package and its associated scripts are designed to analyze interface stability in all-solid-state batteries, computing the thermodynamic stability of solid electrolyte–electrode interfaces from first-principles data. It builds on [pymatgen](http://pymatgen.org/) and the Materials Project.

References:

Yizhou Zhu, Xingfeng He, Yifei Mo\*, "Origin of Outstanding Stability in the Lithium Solid Electrolyte Materials: Insights from Thermodynamic Analyses Based on First-Principles Calculations", [***ACS Applied Materials & Interfaces***, 7, 23685-23693 (2015)](http://dx.doi.org/10.1021/acsami.5b07517)

Yizhou Zhu, Xingfeng He, Yifei Mo\*, "First Principles Study on Electrochemical and Chemical Stability of the Solid Electrolyte-Electrode Interfaces in All-Solid-State Li-ion Batteries", [***Journal of Materials Chemistry A***, 4, 3253-3266 (2016)](http://dx.doi.org/10.1039/C5TA08574H)

Yizhou Zhu, Xingfeng He, Yifei Mo\*, "Strategies Based on Nitride Materials Chemistry to Stabilize Li Metal Anode", [***Advanced Science***, 1600517 (2017)](http://dx.doi.org/10.1002/advs.201600517)
