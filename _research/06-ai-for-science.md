While powerful, AI/ML methods are often viewed as black boxes. We treat AI/ML as a research assistant, not a replacement for physical understanding:

* **Understandable AI.** We use AI/ML in ways that let us understand the resulting models in terms of the underlying physics. Our Density of Atomistic States (DOAS) framework turns AI-driven analysis of complex, disordered atomistic dynamics into physically interpretable descriptors, and our unsupervised learning approach reveals physical commonalities in the structures of newly discovered ion conductors. <span class="citation">[*Angew. Chem. Int. Ed.* 62, e202215544 (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.202215544); [*Nat. Commun.* 10, 5260 (2019)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-019-13214-1)</span>
* **Physical insights by AI.** We leverage AI/ML to reveal new physical mechanisms that weren't accessible before. DOAS uncovers physical insights into the frustration mechanisms that govern ion transport, while our unsupervised learning approach surfaces the structural commonalities shared by newly discovered conductors. <span class="citation">[*Angew. Chem. Int. Ed.* 62, e202215544 (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.202215544); [*Nat. Commun.* 10, 5260 (2019)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-019-13214-1)</span>
* **Materials discovery by AI.** We accelerate materials discovery using AI/ML, including unsupervised learning to prioritize candidates without labeled property data. <span class="citation">[*Nat. Commun.* 10, 5260 (2019)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-019-13214-1)</span>
* **Reliable & trustworthy AI.** We rigorously test the AI/ML methods we use, so we can trust the tools and the results. We showed that standard error metrics for machine learning interatomic potentials (MLIPs) are insufficient to guarantee accurate atomistic dynamics, proposed rare-event-based evaluation metrics instead, and benchmarked thousands of MLIP models to reveal inherent trade-offs between properties. <span class="citation">[*npj Comput. Mater.* 9, 174 (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41524-023-01123-3); [*npj Comput. Mater.* 10, 159 (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41524-024-01333-3); [*Acta Mater.* 268, 119742 (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.actamat.2024.119742)</span>
