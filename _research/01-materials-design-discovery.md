We design and discover materials through high-throughput computation combined with machine learning and AI ([***Advanced Energy Materials*** 9, 1902078 (2019)](https://doi.org/10.1002/aenm.201902078); [***Nature Communications***, 10, 5260 (2019)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-019-13214-1); [***Nature Communications***, 14, 7615 (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-023-43436-3); [***Advanced Energy Materials*** 10, 2002356 (2020)](https://doi.org/10.1002/aenm.202002356)).

Unlike computational studies that consider only a single property, our materials design and discovery accounts for the full, comprehensive set of aspects that determine whether a material actually works in application:

* **Synthesizability** ([***Journal of the American Chemical Society*** 140, 17290-17296 (2018)](https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.8b11029))
* **Phase stability** ([***Joule*** 2, 2016-2046 (2018)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.joule.2018.08.017))
* **Electrochemical stability** ([***ACS Applied Materials & Interfaces***, 7, 23685-23693 (2015)](http://dx.doi.org/10.1021/acsami.5b07517); [***Journal of Materials Chemistry A***, 4, 3253-3266 (2016)](http://dx.doi.org/10.1039/C5TA08574H); [***Advanced Science***, 1600517 (2017)](http://dx.doi.org/10.1002/advs.201600517))
* **Interface stability and compatibility**, essential for materials to work in a device, including potential coatings to make the materials work ([***ACS Applied Materials & Interfaces***, 7, 23685-23693 (2015)](http://dx.doi.org/10.1021/acsami.5b07517); [***Journal of Materials Chemistry A***, 4, 3253-3266 (2016)](http://dx.doi.org/10.1039/C5TA08574H); [***Advanced Science***, 1600517 (2017)](http://dx.doi.org/10.1002/advs.201600517); [***ACS Energy Letters*** 4, 2444-2451 (2019)](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsenergylett.9b01703); [***Energy Storage Materials*** 41, 571-580 (2021)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ensm.2021.06.027))
* **Thermal stability**, relevant to safety and processing ([***Joule*** 4(4), 812-821 (2020)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.joule.2020.03.012))
* **Moisture stability**, relevant to cost and processing ([***Angewandte Chemie Int. Ed.*** 59, 17472 (2020)](https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.202007621))
