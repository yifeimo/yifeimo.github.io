Materials with exceptional properties often involve novel, complex physical mechanisms, and we aim to unravel them at the atomistic level — including disordering, frustration, super-ionic conduction, and non-linear dynamics.

Using techniques like the Density of Atomistic States (DOAS), we reveal these intricacies at the atomic level to guide the design of novel materials.
